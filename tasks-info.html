Tasks

Tasks is a private, locally operated task and calendar application maintained by Jules Jacobs for personal use. It brings email follow-ups and calendar events into one workspace. It is not offered as a public service.

Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026.

With the account owner's authorization, Tasks uses read-only Google access to identify the connected account, read the Gmail Primary inbox, and read subscribed calendars and their events. The integration cannot send or modify Google mail or calendar events.

The background sync stores email headers, message identifiers, attachment names and sizes, and calendar details needed to display events and link them to tasks. It does not retain email bodies or attachment contents during automatic sync. The owner can separately retain selected messages and documents as task evidence.

Task data, calendar data, evidence and backups are stored on the owner's computer. OAuth refresh credentials are stored in the computer's credential store. This public information page has no access to that data.

The background integration communicates with Google to retrieve data. It does not sell Google data, send it to advertising services, or use it to train AI models. The owner may choose to share selected context with an assistant when working on a task; this is separate from automatic synchronization.

Records and backups remain available until removed by the owner. Google access can be revoked in the Google Account connections settings; revocation stops future access but does not delete previously saved local records or backups.

Tasks' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

For questions about this application, contact its maintainer through julesjacobs.com.
