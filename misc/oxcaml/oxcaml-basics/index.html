OxCaml Basics

A three-hour course for Jane Street interns and engineers about safe high-performance Okskaml: checked parallelism, stack allocation, and control over runtime representation.

  1. 00

    Why OxCaml exists

    Why ordinary OCaml is not always enough for high-performance code, and which OxCaml features this course will cover.

    10 min · motivation

  2. 01

    Parallelism: contention and portability

    How to run callbacks in parallel without data races when they receive explicit arguments or close over mutable state.

    80 min · parallelism

  3. 02

    Locality and stack allocation

    How local values, stack_, and exclave_ help control heap allocation and lifetime.

    45 min · allocation

  4. 03

    Value layouts and unboxing

    Uniform OCaml values first, then the costs of boxes and pointers, then layouts, unboxed values, or_null, and a short note on templates.

    45 min · representation

    Lecture slides
