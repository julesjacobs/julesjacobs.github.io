A three-hour course for Jane Street interns and engineers about safe high-performance OCaml: checked parallelism, stack allocation and zero allocation, and control over runtime representation.

  1. 00

    Why OxCaml exists

    Why ordinary OCaml is not always enough for high-performance code, and which OxCaml features this course will cover.

    10 min · motivation

  2. 01

    Parallelism: contention and portability

    Fork/join, detached spawn, mutable-state safety, portability modes, contention modes, and closure boundaries.

    70 min · parallelism

  3. 02

    Locality, stack allocation, and zeroalloc

    How local values, stack_, exclave_, and zero-allocation checks help control heap allocation and latency.

    40 min · allocation

  4. 03

    Value layouts and unboxing

    Uniform OCaml values first, then the costs of boxes and pointers, then layouts, unboxed values, or_null, and a short note on templates.

    60 min · representation
